01 / Our purpose

A health question shouldn’t feel like a dead end.

Finding the right information can be difficult when a family is already worried. Avinya Care Foundation brings healthcare information, care navigation and community enquiries into one place.

We help you understand the available next steps. Assistance, appointments and funding are confirmed individually, so you can make decisions with clear expectations.
