FEATURED CARE INITIATIVES

Empowering patients & communities.

Explore our core operational pillars designed to detect cancer early and support families throughout their health journey.

01 / AWARENESS & KNOWLEDGE

Know earlier.

Knowledge is the first and strongest defense against cancer. By educating communities on early symptoms, risk factors, and routine self-examinations, we empower individuals to take control of their health long before clinical complications arise.

02 / EARLY SCREENING

Don't wait for symptoms.

Most early-stage cancers produce zero physical pain or symptoms. Our mobile diagnostic screening initiatives bring state-of-the-art screening closer to communities, making routine checkups accessible, affordable, and stress-free.

03 / HUMAN CARE

Care doesn't end with diagnosis.

Navigating treatment options, medical terminology, and emotional strain can feel overwhelming. Our trained care navigators walk alongside patients and families every single step of the way.

THE CONTINUUM OF CARE

Every step of your health journey.

Built on human compassion and clinical excellence, Avinya Care guides individuals through every milestone — keeping hope clear, accessible, and supported at every stage.

01 / STAGE

Awareness & Education

02 / STAGE

Early Detection

Mobile screening vans, mammograms, and clinical checkups catching signs long before physical symptoms appear.

03 / STAGE

Clinical Navigation

Helping patients interpret biopsy results, connect with leading oncologists, and navigate financial aid & 80G grants.

04 / STAGE

Treatment Support

Bedside companion volunteers, clinical nutrition management, and emotional support for family caregivers.

05 / STAGE

Survivorship & Hope

Post-treatment rehabilitation, ongoing surveillance monitoring, and thriving survivor mentorship circles.
