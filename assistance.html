BEFORE YOU REACH OUT

Support starts with understanding.

What you can ask about

Care navigation, diagnostic booking, financial assistance enquiries and community initiatives. Ask the team to confirm service area, eligibility, available funding and whether a service is free, subsidized or paid.

How to request help

Call 744 744 1116 or contact us on WhatsApp. A phone call is an alternative to using email or an online form. Share your name, callback number, location and the kind of help needed. Do not send full medical reports or identity documents in an initial enquiry.

Review and follow-up

The team will need to confirm what assistance is available and any supporting documents. A request is not an approval or a confirmed appointment. Ask for a reference and expected response time; quote it when following up. Funding and treatment outcomes cannot be guaranteed.

Clinical responsibility

Tests, consultations, diagnosis and treatment are the responsibility of the clinician or laboratory providing them. Ask who will review results and arrange further care. A normal test result does not rule out every illness.
