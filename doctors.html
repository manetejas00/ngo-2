Privacy notice

Website data and how to ask about it. Updated 24 October 2026.

Information provided through forms

Forms request contact details, enquiry text, donation interest or booking details. Healthcare booking may also request age, gender, collection address and appointment information. Provide only what is necessary for the request. Avoid unrelated medical records or financial credentials.

Use and service providers

Details are used to handle requests, arrange appointments, communicate confirmations and maintain operational records. Booking details may be shared with the assigned clinician or diagnostic provider. Email delivery and hosting providers process information needed to operate the service.

Analytics

If configured, website analytics measures general page use and events. Form fields should not be sent to analytics. This update excludes diagnostic detail pages and admin pages from its manual page-view events and omits query strings and fragments. Your browser and network may still share technical connection information with providers.

Retention and privacy requests

Ask the foundation about the retention period, service providers and legal requirements applicable to your records. To request access, correction, deletion or raise a concern, contact the foundation. Identity verification or statutory retention may apply. No fixed retention or deletion timeframe is asserted here.

Sensitive communications

Do not put medical reports, PAN, passwords or banking codes in public comments or ordinary initial enquiries. Ask for the appropriate secure route before sending sensitive documents.

Website terms and service information

Updated 24 October 2026.

Information and healthcare

Website information supports enquiries and is not a diagnosis or individualized medical advice. A clinician should guide testing, interpret results and arrange follow-up. Screening, diagnosis and monitoring serve different purposes.

Requests and appointments

Submitting a form records a request; it does not guarantee approval, funding, a slot or service availability. Confirm the provider, location, preparation, price, additional charges, report turnaround and cancellation or rescheduling arrangements before attending.

Contributions

Read the donation guide. An interest acknowledgement is not proof of payment. Verify project documents, approved payment arrangements and any applicable tax approval before contributing.

Contact

For corrections, complaints or questions, call 744 744 1116 or email info@avinyacarefoundation.org.

Documents and transparency

Ask for evidence before relying on a claim or choosing a project.

Foundation documents

Request current registration records, exact legal identity, applicable tax approvals, CSR documentation and any available annual reports or audited financial statements. Publication and validity of these records are not verified by this website update.

Impact reporting

Verified surgery counts and patient outcome claims are not published here while supporting records and publication permissions are unavailable. Recorded contributions are not the same as funds disbursed or patients treated.

Proposed projects

Hospital, NICU, dialysis, ambulance and camp initiatives shown on the crowdfunding page are proposals. Locations, partners, approvals, costs, dates and operating capacity require confirmation. Images are illustrative. A planning amount is not evidence of the cost of a complete hospital or service.

Before a restricted-purpose contribution

Ask for scope, budget, approvals, milestones, responsible contact and reporting arrangements. Confirm what happens if a proposal changes, is delayed, cannot proceed or receives excess funds.

Questions or concerns

For patient, donor or privacy concerns, email info@avinyacarefoundation.org or call 744 744 1116. Include an existing request reference where available and ask for the appropriate foundation contact.
